## Bibliographic record

- **Title:** *Monedas y Billetes de Colombia* — subtitle *Cien Años Banco de la República 1923–2023*.
- **Author / editor:** Pedro Pablo Hernández (compiler); **8th edition, 2023**.
- **ISBN (print, from title verso):** 978-958-49-7741-0.
- **Public citation on the site:** Numista literature **[L100183](https://en.numista.com/catalogue/literature.php?id=100183)** (Hernández, *Monedas y Billetes de Colombia*). Do **not** cite this repo path.
- **Cover series:** “Cien Años Banco de la República” is the 1923–2023 centennial frame. It does **not** make this an official Banco de la República publication.

## Numbering

- This edition uses **Cód.** (catalogue codes) on coins and notes (e.g. **Cód. 11** Santa Marta 1820 ¼ real; **Cód. 1109** Banco de Riohacha 5 pesos 1 Jan 1883; **Cód. 1134** Banco Hipotecario 5 pesos ABNC).
- Older Notofilia copy citing **Hernández 91 / 95 / 96 / 199 / 201** follows **Numista** concordance to an earlier Hernández listing. Do **not** overwrite those H# with 2023 Cód. unless Numista is updated.

## What to take / what to leave

**Take (facts, already used or available for copy):**

- Independence paper: Hernández lists **2 reales 1812** at **Mompós** as the first Colombian notes in his catalogue (unique known in his listing), then **1 real 1813** signed Germán Gutiérrez de Piñeres; ordinary paper; 100 reales of this paper traded for 16; possible **1814**. Keep Banrepcultural’s **1813** 1-real lead in parallel — do not drop it.
- Gran Colombia remainders: **never issued**, unsigned, **Peter Maverick** (New York); 6 / 12 / 18 / 30 pesos in **sheets of six**; salt at Zipaquirá, Nemocón, Tausa; Bolívar vs Santander in the vignettes.
- **1922 Junta de Conversión** (Ley 5, Decreto 165, escritura 206 Notaría 3ª): treasury bonds overstamped before BanRep **23 July 1923**.
- Banco Hipotecario: founded Bogotá **1880s**, **liquidated before 1914**; 5 pesos ABNC seated woman / horses = **Cód. 1134**. Distinguish Banco de la Mutualidad (1914) and Banco del Pacífico.
- Banco de Riohacha: founded **1885**, liquidated **1888**; circulating **5 pesos 1 Jan 1883** Bolívar centre = **Cód. 1109**. Notofilia’s piece is **ABNCo proofs**, not that circulating row.
- Santa Marta **1820** royalist copper ¼ real **SM** = **Cód. 11** (already on the coin record).
- Specimen / prueba / **mariposa** (miscut) annex definitions — glossary only, no plates.

**Leave:**

- All **$** / **US $** price columns and dealer ads (ASOCOPA, etc.).
- Plate serials that are **not** Notofilia holdings (e.g. 20.000 pesos **29 Aug 2017** plate **AC53513901** / series **AE** **675NF** — collection note is **AF51355141**, González White **BG# NF20-6** series **AF**).
- Invented printage from a Cód. row.
