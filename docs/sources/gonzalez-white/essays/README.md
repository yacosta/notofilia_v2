# González White essays and notes (2012–2015)

Short pieces by Bernardo González White (`begow`), mostly circulated as Word files among collectors. Use them for **design history, varieties, and dated mintage tables**. Prefer BanRep `docs/sources/banrep/` when a year total disagrees and the essay is older; prefer these essays for varieties (dots, mint marks, steel vs alpaca) that the BanRep spreadsheet does not split.

© Bernardo González White. Research copies. **Do not** publish embedded photos or 2012–2015 price asides on the site.

Search the `.txt` extracts; open the `.docx` for plates.

## Notes (Colombia)

| File | Date | Topic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `2012-04-03-billete-1000-pesos-gaitan` | 3 Apr 2012 | $1.000 Gaitán note (first date 7 Aug 2001, circulation 2002, 140×70 mm, 9 issues; from 2006 130×65 mm, 14 issues through 10 Jun 2011 / circ. 27 Feb 2012). Designer José Antonio Suárez. Discusses the crowd figure often identified as Fidel Castro on the face (BanRep presentation booklet p. 12) and the reverse montage from 23 Sep 1945. Author’s circulation estimate ~2.3 billion notes over ten years — treat as an essay figure, not BanRep. |

## Coins (Colombia)

| File | Date | Topic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `2012-02-19-monedas-50-pesos` | 19 Feb 2012 | $50 1989–2011: metal (alpaca vs nickel-plated steel), varieties (dots 66/72, thorax, date, rotated dies). Extra tables in the same file: **nueva familia $20** 2004–2008 and **$1.000** 1996–1998. Notes that some web lists invent 1995–1996 $50 dates. |
| `2012-03-28-monedas-500-pesos` | 28 Mar 2012 | Bimetallic $500 1993–2012 mintages. 1996 and 2011 said to exist from Ibagué and Germany; Ibagué did not certify those. Expected mintmarks `I` / `G`. |
| `2012-04-03-moneda-200-pesos` | 3 Apr 2012 | $200 from 1 Jul 1994. Design: Dicken Castro after Antonio Grass “volante de huso” / Quimbaya motif (BanRep: four bird heads; some texts say fish). Edge legend. Table 1993–2012; 1995 “punto en el centro”. Castro also designed 1968 gold commemoratives and the 1983 Bolívar stamp. |
| `2015-10-23-moneda-santa-laura` | 23 Oct 2015 | Santa Laura Montoya $5.000 commemorative: available 23 Oct 2015; first delivery 100,000; authorized 500,000; Cu75/Ni25; 35 mm; 21.75 g; reeded; Ibagué. BanRep `dye_mon_produccion` 2015 column 5.000 pesos is `500.000 3/` (footnote: this coin). Do not treat 100,000 as the full authorized mintage unless writing about the first delivery only. |
