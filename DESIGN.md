# Notofilia brand identity

Source of truth for the Astro + Tailwind rebuild. Adapted from Notofilia Brand Identity v1.0 (August 2026).

## Essence

**El archivo vivo del papel moneda.** One word: Curatorial.

Spanish first. Nothing is for sale. The interface stays quiet so the artifacts carry the color.

## Tokens

Use the Tailwind v4 theme in `src/styles/global.css`:

- Intaglio green: `brand-950` `#0B2E1F` · `brand-800` `#175239` (primary) · `brand-100` `#DEEFE6`
- Escudo gold: `gold-700` `#8C6A1D` (text on light) · `gold-500` `#C9A227` (decorative only)
- Paper & ink: `paper-50` `#FAF7F0` · `paper-100` `#F2EDE0` · `paper-300` `#DDD4BF` · `ink-900` `#1C1A15` · `ink-600` `#5C574B`
- Seal red: `seal-700` `#9B2C2C` (errors / specimen emphasis)

Type: Fraunces (display), Source Serif 4 (body), Inter (UI), IBM Plex Mono (stats, catalog data).

## Wordmark

**NOTOFILIA** in the display serif. Running text: Notofilia. Discipline: *notafilia*. Lockup: NOTOFILIA — Numismática y Notafilia.

## Do / don’t

- Warm ivory backgrounds, never pure white or cold gray.
- Mono + middle dots for the signature stat line.
- Gold-500 never as text on light.
- Non-commercial disclaimer in every footer.
